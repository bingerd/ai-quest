# The three levers on cost

Most AI bills are not large because the model is expensive. They are large because the same tokens are sent again and again, at full price, for work nobody is waiting for.

<ConceptCard title="Order decides whether caching is possible at all" icon="🧱">
  Put tools, system prompt and documents first, conversation and the new message last. One volatile block near the front makes everything after it uncacheable.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptReveal prompt="Your calls are 20 minutes apart. Which cache lifetime?">
  The one-hour lifetime. Its write costs 2x instead of 1.25x, but with a five-minute lifetime the entry expires between calls, so you pay a write every single time and never get a 0.1x read. Match the lifetime to the traffic.
</ConceptReveal>
