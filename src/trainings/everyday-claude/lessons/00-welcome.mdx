# Get more out of Claude, without learning to code.

This training is for everyone in a consulting firm who works in documents: steering decks and status reports, proposals and credentials, account plans and client summaries, candidate briefs and interview notes. Sales, HR, account managers, delivery teams and the partners who sign it all off. In about fifteen minutes you will:

- see why long chats get slow and use up your limit, and fix it
- build a request that gets a usable first draft instead of a generic one
- decide what belongs in a project, what belongs in a single chat, and what should never go in at all — including your client's data
- build a deck you would put in front of a client, end to end
