# Real slides, not just text

You do not have to copy text into PowerPoint yourself.

- **File creation.** Claude can create real PowerPoint (.pptx), Word, Excel and PDF files for you to download. It needs *Code execution and file creation* turned on under Settings › Capabilities. Creating files uses more of your usage limit than a normal reply.
- **Artifacts.** For drafts you want to look at and tweak, Claude can make an artifact: a deck, document or small tool that opens next to the chat and can be shared by link.
- **Claude for PowerPoint.** On paid plans, an add-in works inside PowerPoint itself. It reads your slide master, fonts and colours, can change a single slide without touching the rest, and turns bullet points into editable charts.

<ConceptCard title="Anthropic's own advice for decks" icon="📐">
  Apply your company template *before* generating content. Say exactly which slide or element to change. Review every change, check it matches your brand, and keep a human review before anything goes to a client or the board.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptReveal prompt="Someone sends you a slide deck from outside the company and you ask Claude to improve it. Anything to watch for?">
  Yes. Files from people you do not know can contain hidden text that tries to steer the AI. Anthropic warns about this for the PowerPoint add-in. Treat untrusted files with the same care you would give an email attachment, and review what changed.
</ConceptReveal>
