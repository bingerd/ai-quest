# Real slides, not just text

You do not have to copy text into PowerPoint yourself.

- **File creation.** Claude can create real PowerPoint (.pptx), Word, Excel and PDF files for you to download. It needs *Code execution and file creation* turned on under Settings › Capabilities. Creating files uses more of your usage limit than a normal reply.
- **Artifacts.** For drafts you want to look at and tweak, Claude can make an artifact: a deck, document or small tool that opens next to the chat and can be shared by link.
- **Claude for PowerPoint.** On paid plans, an add-in works inside PowerPoint itself. It reads your slide master, fonts and colours, can change a single slide without touching the rest, and turns bullet points into editable charts.

<ConceptCard title="Anthropic's own advice for decks" icon="📐">
  Apply your firm's template *before* generating content. Say exactly which slide or element to change. Review every change, check it matches your brand, and keep a human review before anything reaches a client.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptReveal prompt="Almost every file you open came from outside your firm. Does that make them all unsafe?">
  No, and that is the point: "it came from outside" is not a useful filter when client exports, vendor decks and candidate CVs *are* your working day. The real rule is narrower. A document can contain text written to be read by an AI rather than by you — hidden instructions telling it to change figures, ignore your brief or reveal what else is in the project. Anthropic warns about this for the PowerPoint add-in. So treat instructions found inside any document as *content to report*, never as orders to follow, and always read what actually changed.
</ConceptReveal>
