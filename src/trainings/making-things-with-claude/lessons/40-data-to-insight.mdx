# Numbers you can defend

A wrong number in a deck is not like a typo. A typo makes you look careless for a second. A wrong
number gets decided on, and then someone finds it.

Claude can genuinely help with data work. With *Code execution and file creation* switched on, it
creates real .xlsx, .pptx, .docx and PDF files, up to 30 MB, on every plan including Free. It can
take a messy export and give you back a clean sheet and a chart. What it cannot do is tell you
which numbers matter enough to check.

<ConceptCard title="Suspect the reasonable-sounding ones" icon="🎯" tone="warn">
  An obviously mad number gets caught by everyone. The dangerous figure is the plausible one with
  no source — "industry churn averages 7%" — because nobody questions it until it is in front of a
  client. Plausible is not the same as verified.
</ConceptCard>

<ConceptCard title="What costs you, and what does not" icon="⏳">

  drop the effort level for routine work.
</ConceptCard>
