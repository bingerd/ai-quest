# Welcome, agent.

Your mission, should you choose to accept it: learn to manage AI **context and tokens** like a pro, in about twenty minutes.

Along the way you will:

- see text turn into tokens and watch the cost meter move
- squeeze a bloated context window until it fits
- run **Context Surgeon** and **Token Heist**, two challenges about choosing the right information
- pick a model for a real-world constraint, and live with the consequences
- steer AI adoption across a 5,000-person organisation

<ConceptCard title="Everything here is simulated" icon="🧪">
  No AI model is ever called. Every number you see is produced by a deterministic simulator built to teach concepts, not to measure a real model. That means you can experiment as much as you like. Nothing costs anything.
