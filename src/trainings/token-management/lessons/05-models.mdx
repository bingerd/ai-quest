# Match the tool to the task

Vendors ship <VendorTerm concept="model" /> in tiers: small and fast, balanced, and large "reasoning" models. The tiers differ in three ways that matter to you:

| | Small | Balanced | Large |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handles complex tasks | poorly | well | very well |
| Cost per token | lowest | mid | highest |
| Speed | fastest | mid | slowest |

<ConceptReveal prompt="Is the cheapest model the right default?">
  No. Neither is the most capable one. The right model is the **cheapest one that clears your quality bar** within your latency and budget limits. For trivial bulk work that is often the small model. For nuanced, high-stakes work it may be the large one, and paying for it is the efficient choice.
</ConceptReveal>
