# From one prompt to five thousand people

Everything so far was about a single request. At organisation scale, the same ideas become **policy**:

- **Context discipline** decides whether the AI budget is spent on answers or on noise.
- **Model tiering** decides whether you pay premium prices for trivial work.
- **Tool scoping** decides how much damage an agent can do when it makes a mistake.
- **Data classification** decides what may enter a prompt at all.
